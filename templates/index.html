Reference drawing optional

Show the model an example of the inking style you want: it copies the line weights and the stippling, never the shapes. In our benchmark a reference did not improve GPT Image 2 on average (88% usable drawings with and without), so use it to match a particular house style and check the results. The reference is also sent to the provider.

Additional instructions

Text added after the standard prompt, for example “the drawing has a scale bar: keep it”. The standard prompt cannot be changed. Custom text makes results harder to reproduce.
