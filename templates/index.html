About PyPotteryInk

Lorenzo Cardarelli

v{{ version }}

PyPotteryInk is a Generative AI tool designed for translating archaeological pottery drawings into publication-ready illustrations.

DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENT

When publishing or presenting results that use PyPotteryInk, please include:

  • 1 The version of PyPotteryInk used (e.g. v{{ version }})
  • 2 The specific model used (e.g. 10k Model or 6h-MCG Model)
  • 3 The number of images processed
Suggested citation format:
"This research utilized PyPotteryInk (version {{ version }}) for the AI-assisted translation of [number] pottery drawings. PyPotteryInk is a generative AI tool developed by Lorenzo Cardarelli (https://github.com/lrncrd/PyPotteryInk)."

System Requirements Check

This tool requires significant computational resources. Please verify that your local hardware meets the scientific processing requirements.

Recommended Specifications:

  • GPU: NVIDIA with at least 8GB VRAM (minimum 4GB) or Apple Silicon (MPS)
  • CPU: 4+ modern cores (Intel i5/AMD Ryzen 5 or better)
  • RAM: 16GB (minimum 8GB)
  • Storage: Fast SSD (NVMe recommended)
